# Plain writing

**Invariant:** the owner is not an engineer. Lead with the result, in plain words, and say only what is true.

## Write

- What happened, what it does, or what the reader should do, first. Mechanics after, and only when needed to decide.
- Concrete names, numbers, paths, UI labels and evidence instead of adjectives.
- One thought per sentence; vary the length. Same word for the same thing (`CONTEXT.md`).
- Statuses tell the truth: never "done", "ready" or "passed" because something stopped. Say what was not tested.
- In the app: the owner's words from `CONTEXT.md` ("Plan the work", "Check it", "Computer"), not internal names (Supervisor, Verifier, Node, workstation).
- Explain a necessary technical term once, in a few words.

## Cut

- Openings and closings: praise, "Great question", "I hope this helps", summaries of what was just said.
- Filler: "in order to", "it is important to note", stacked hedges.
- AI vocabulary when a plain word works: delve, leverage, utilize, robust, seamless, facilitate, pivotal.
- Metaphors when the mechanism has a name: substrate, harness, scaffolding, flywheel, north star.
- Decorative headings, emoji, title case, bold on every line, forced lists of three.
- Investigation diaries: what you tried belongs in the trace, not the text.

## Self-check

Read it once as the owner on a phone. Anything vague, stiff or that needs a second read: rewrite it.
